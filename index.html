Tugboat

Tugboat does the work. You press Send.

An AI assistant that runs on your own computer, day and night. It reads your email, WhatsApp and Signal as they come in, and tells you the few things that matter.

Then it gets the work ready, up to the last click:

  • the reply, already written
  • the form, already filled
  • the booking, already found
  • the bill, already chased

It asks you only before it sends something in your name or spends your money. Everything else it just does, and tells you what it did.

It can't be talked into sending

Nothing goes out in your name and nothing is paid without your tap. The part that reads your messages cannot send anything.

The part that reads: Read ✓ · Send ✗ · Browse ✗ · Write files ✗

Why a tugboat?

A great ship can cross an ocean but cannot dock itself. The last stretch belongs to a small, stubborn boat that knows the harbour. It pushes the ship alongside, and the captain gives the final order.

Tugboat does that with your day. It brings everything alongside. You tie it off.

Can I try it?

Not yet. Today it runs on one PC, its maker's. A version you can install is being built now, Windows first. It will be open source.
