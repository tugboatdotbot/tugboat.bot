How it works

Every message read. Only what matters reaches you, with the work already done.

  1. It reads everything as it arrives

    Email, WhatsApp and Signal, day and night, on your own computer. Newsletters, receipts and noise stay out of your way.

  2. It gets the work ready

    A reply written for you. A follow-up for someone who never answered. A form on a website filled in, stopped at the last button.

  3. You check it and tap Send

    Your morning list shows the few things that matter, each one ready. When a website needs a code, it asks you on your phone and carries on.

Safe by design

It can't be talked into sending.

  • You press Send.

    Nothing goes out in your name and nothing is paid without your tap.

  • The reader can't act.

    The part that reads your messages can read, and nothing else: it can't send, browse or write files.

  • Tricks get flagged.

    An email that tries to give it orders is shown to you as suspicious, not obeyed.

  • No Tugboat server.

    Your mail and messages stay on your computer. We never see them.

About

Independent and open source

Tugboat isn't a cloud service. It's software you run yourself, and we use it every day on our own email and messages. What we learn there is what we build next.

There's no Tugboat server, so your mail and messages never reach us. No ads, and your data is never sold or shared. The only thing we keep is the email you leave here, to tell you when it's ready.

Why a tugboat? A great ship can cross an ocean but can't dock itself. A small, stubborn boat brings it alongside, and the captain gives the final order. Tugboat does that with your day.

Questions

Good to know

What leaves my computer?

Nothing of yours leaves your computer, except the text sent to your AI company for a job, and the words of a web search.

Does it send email for me?

It writes the reply. You read it and press Send. It never sends in your name or spends your money without your tap.

Which AI does it use?

Your own account with an AI company. Today that's Claude, by Anthropic. ChatGPT and others are planned. You pay the AI company directly; Tugboat itself is free.

Does my computer have to stay on?

Yes. Tugboat works while the computer is on and awake, so a desktop that stays on works best. A laptop that sleeps will pause it. That's why we're planning a small ready-made box.

Mac or Linux?

Windows comes first. The installer already runs on Mac and Linux in our tests; those follow soon after.

Is the code public?

Not yet. We plan to publish it on GitHub around Thanksgiving, free and open source.

When can I try it?
